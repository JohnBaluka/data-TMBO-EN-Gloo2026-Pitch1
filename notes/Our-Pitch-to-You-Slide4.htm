I watched people who genuinely wanted to understand the Bible struggle with something very simple:

Where do I start, and how do I understand what I'm reading?

The problem isn't a lack of Bible content.

We already have Bible apps, audio Bibles, sermons, podcasts, videos, maps, images, timelines, and commentaries.

The problem is that all of those experiences are disconnected.

Nobody had brought them together because we kept thinking about the Bible as a book, an audiobook, a video, or a website.

Our insight was:  Why does it have to be just one?
