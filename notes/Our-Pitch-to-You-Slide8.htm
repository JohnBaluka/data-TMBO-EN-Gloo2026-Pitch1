In this demo, suppose I want to experience Matthew chapter 9.


I can read the Scripture and supporting content at my own pace.

I can listen and follow along while the passage with a narration.

I have the ability to jump ahead and click on a verse to play that verse.

I can still engage with the Scripture like on Bible.com and see the cross references for a given verse.

Or I can watch as the Scripture come alive as a slide show or a video on YouTube.

It is in your control to choose the view that is best optimized for reading or watching the Word of God.


Same Scripture. One experience. You choose how you consume it.
