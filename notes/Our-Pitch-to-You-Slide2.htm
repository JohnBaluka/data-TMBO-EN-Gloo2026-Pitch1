For many people, opening the Bible can feel overwhelming. So what if we gave them a simpler place to begin?

What if we started with Jesus?   His life. His words. His miracles. His story - told (as witnessed by) through Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John.

And what if you could experience that story in the way that best brings the Scripture to life - read the words, hear the story, and watch it unfold in a way that keeps you returning to Jesus's story—all in one place?

That’s The Multimodal Bible.  The same Scripture. New ways to experience it.
